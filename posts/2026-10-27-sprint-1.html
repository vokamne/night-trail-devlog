Sprint 1 – Lighting prototype

Sprint review27 Oct 2026

Goal: check that darkness, lights and shadows work in 2D.

What I did

  • Set up the project (Godot 4.7.2, GitHub)
  • Player movement
  • Day/night cycle
  • Headlamp with battery
  • Campfire with a safe zone
  • Shadows from trees and rocks

Problems & solutions

Night was too dark

Problem
You couldn't see anything without light.

Solution
I made the night lighter and bluer, so trees still show as silhouettes.

The headlamp looked green

Problem
Light multiplies with the surface colour, and the grass was too green.

Solution
I made the light warmer and the grass less saturated.

Hard line at the base of the light beam

Problem
The built-in gradient couldn't fade in near the player, so the beam started with a sharp edge.

Solution
The beam texture is now generated in code with a soft start.

The headlamp was too bright in the daytime

Problem
The headlamp was just as strong in full daylight.

Solution
Lights now get brighter only when it gets dark.

Tree tops were getting shadows from trunks

Problem
Shadows from the trunks fell on the tree tops above them.

Solution
I put the tree tops on a separate light layer that doesn't get shadows.

Tree shadows looked like thin spikes

Problem
Shadows from the trees were long and thin.

Solution
I made the shadow shape wider and the shadows softer.

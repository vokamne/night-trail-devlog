Hi! I am a student at Kingston University. Night Trail is my game project: a 2D top-down adventure in Godot 4 about a hiker, a mountain trail and the light that keeps them safe at night.

This blog follows the project from the first prototype to the final build. After each sprint I write what I did, what went wrong and how I fixed it.

GitHub: vokamne
